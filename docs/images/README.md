# Hardware photographs, 2026-10-13

Photographs supplied by the project owner and copied without modification.
The red jumper markings were already present in the supplied image.

- `two-uno-timestick-setup.png`: two UNO/shield/TimeStick assemblies with a direct Ethernet connection.
- `two-timestick-pps-oscilloscope.png`: the two PPS outputs connected to oscilloscope channels 1 and 2.
- `usb-host-shield-power-jumpers.png`: the tested VS60 shield with three required jumper positions circled.

The [main README](../../README.md#the-working-setup) displays the images with
setup notes and serial samples. Photo labels and scope channels have not been
mapped to the USB serial identities. No numerical PPS offset or calibrated
accuracy is derived from the oscilloscope photograph. `SHA256SUMS` records
the exact supplied PNG bytes.
