tinyPTP paper preparation, ISPCS 2027
Working draft created 2026-10-15; physical PPS follow-up added 2026-10-16. Not submitted or submission-ready.

FILES
- tinyptp-ispcs2027.tex: standalone IEEE conference manuscript, including plots
  and bibliography. No external figure files or bibliography database needed.
- refresh_figures.py: checks archived log hashes, sample counts, and RMS values,
  then refreshes embedded figure coordinates and the physical servo table,
  including raw PPS/serial recomputation after native-export verification.
  Requires Python 3 standard library.

POSITIONING
Primary contribution: implementation and characterization of a small PTP
experimental platform using a USB Ethernet timestamping peripheral with an
8-bit or Cortex-M4F controller. No claim of a new PTP algorithm, first-ever
implementation, calibrated nanosecond accuracy, or universal servo superiority.
The reported quantitative servo campaign is R4. R3 figures are build-resource
results from the matching archived matrix, not a controlled performance baseline.

PHYSICAL PPS FOLLOW-UP, 2026-10-16
- Archive: docs/results/2026-10-16/saleae-pps/.
- Saleae Logic Pro 8, 500 MS/s, CH0 follower and CH1 grandmaster.
- Same-type, approximately equal-length leads and common ground confirmed by user.
- 900.091 s, fixed PI, simultaneous raw serial logs from both R4 boards.
- Two bursts of extra transitions interrupt two high portions, cause undetermined.
- Post-inspection qualification: complete continuous high and preceding low
  durations each 490--510 ms. The low-duration condition was added after the
  final restoration check; it leaves the long-run counts/statistics unchanged.
  Excludes affected cycles and boundary partial pulses; no phase-based rejection.
- 777 post-cutoff rising pairs: mean +14.59 ns, SD 21.74 ns, RMS 26.18 ns,
  range -46 to +64 ns. Positive means follower physical edge is later.
- Simultaneous internal estimates: 781 post-cutoff samples, RMS 26.13 ns.
- No channel/cable correction, traceable calibration, or UTC reference.
- Subsequent 60.095 s same-source check: 61 rising pairs, mean CH1-minus-CH0
  +0.230 ns, range 0 to +2 ns; no extra transitions. No correction applied.
- Restoration check: 30 s, follower remains locked; five CH1 notches near
  rising edges. All raw and qualified diagnostic data are archived separately.
- Clean repeats and disturbance investigation pending. The same-source check
  is empirical, not traceable calibration, and changes output loading.
- This is one PI observation, not physical evidence ranking the five servos.

FIVE-CONTROLLER SALEAE CAMPAIGN, 2026-10-16
- Archive: docs/results/2026-10-16/saleae-servos/.
- Completed five 300 s captures and one 900.091 s PI record with simultaneous
  native PPS recordings and raw serial logs; complete manifest verified.
- 2,334 accepted exchanges in raw serial logs, no sample gaps or reported
  exchange errors. All 1,680 post-cutoff serial samples are in s3.
- Qualified rising-edge RMS: PI 9.94 ns (177 pairs), regression 6.22 ns (170),
  adaptive PI 10.16 ns (155), Kalman 11.55 ns (159), PLL/FLL 17.77 ns (176).
  Longer PI: 38.44 ns (758), simultaneous internal RMS 38.16 ns (780).
- This campaign is additional evidence, not a replacement for the initial
  26.18 ns PI record. The PI observations must not be pooled.
- Identical firmware hashes to the original R4 campaign, fixed sequential
  order, one observation per condition, no controlled initial phase.
- Same instrument, channel mapping, 500 MS/s and threshold preset. The earlier
  high/preceding-low qualification rule was declared before outcome analysis.
- This campaign applies 120 s from Saleae capture start, including approximately
  aligned serial observations. The older serial-only analysis used time from
  first accepted exchange; do not describe their windows as identical.
- CH1 extra transitions remain unexplained. Qualified sample counts differ,
  making comparisons conditional on different exclusions. Keep all raw edges.
- Physical startup plots do not establish identical step responses. The final
  in-band suffix diagnostic requires 30 s with no gap above 1.5 s; excluded
  pulses can prevent a result without implying servo failure.
- Same-source checks do not remove loading differences or prove traceable
  calibration. No new cable/channel correction or UTC reference is introduced.
- Repeated randomized trials, signal-path diagnosis, and the controlled
  disturbances below remain outstanding. The new capture does not repeat R3
  or Linux-peer topology experiments.

PRIORITY MEASUREMENTS BEFORE SUBMISSION
1. Repeat the independent PPS comparison with verified measurement paths,
   simultaneous serial captures, and investigation of the extra transitions.
   Record counter/scope model, calibration, input thresholds, cable delays,
   channel skew, termination, warm-up, and reference-clock arrangement.
   Reverse channels, swap cables, and exchange endpoint roles to investigate
   fixed measurement and link asymmetries. Report uncertainty and distinguish
   relative phase from UTC accuracy.
2. Repeated controller comparison with predeclared parameters and windows.
   Randomize run order in blocks, use a common warm-up and step policy, record
   ambient/device temperature, and repeat on more than one endpoint pair if
   available. Start with at least ten runs per controller as a planning target;
   adjust repetition count using observed run-to-run variability. Treat runs,
   not correlated one-second samples, as the replication unit.
3. Controlled transients.
   Apply identical signed phase/frequency perturbations to each controller;
   report initial error, overshoot, settling criterion, saturation, and recovery.
   Include a baseline with the same fractional integral arithmetic to isolate
   gain scheduling from precision and interval-handling changes.
4. Longer stability and recovery.
   Collect multi-hour PPS and serial records before choosing meaningful TDEV,
   MTIE, or Allan-deviation averaging ranges. Test packet loss, changing traffic,
   disconnects, reboot/reinitialization, and changed link paths separately.
5. Resource and actuation characterization.
   Measure stack high-water use and timing of hardware frequency updates. Run
   both MCU platforms under matched conditions before claiming a speed or timing
   benefit attributable to the processor.

The October 16 archives distinguish completed qualified PPS observations from
proposed controlled and replicated studies. The priority measurements above
remain proposed work, not completed results. Existing
records are sufficient for a feasibility draft but not a calibrated accuracy
claim or a statistically supported ranking of the five controllers.
